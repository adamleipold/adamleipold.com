# AdamLeipold.com — Cinematic Version-One Brief for Sable

**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Creative partner:** Sable  
**UI owner:** Fable  
**Implementation:** Lisa / Codex  
**Target:** Publish tonight on Vercel, then connect `adamleipold.com` through GoDaddy

## The assignment

Create the visual direction for a cinematic, fabulous first version of
AdamLeipold.com.

This is not a conventional personal-brand site, résumé, portfolio grid, church
site, blog template, or social-network imitation. It is the opening scene of a
living public story: Adam's technology, faith, survival, curiosity, writing,
and becoming belong to one continuous life.

The first release is intentionally small:

1. One immersive homepage.
2. Two permanent authored pages.
3. No conventional navigation.
4. No empty sections or nonfunctional destinations.

Everything may evolve later. Version one should feel complete rather than
trying to predict the final site.

## Emotional objective

The visitor should feel that they have entered a living world, not opened a
document.

The experience should be:

- Cinematic without becoming theatrical.
- Sacred without looking denominational.
- Technological without looking corporate or cyberpunk.
- Personal without becoming a biography dump.
- Mysterious enough to invite exploration, clear enough to feel intentional.

The emotional center is: **many paths, one life; everything connects.**

## The central image and palette

The visual seed is Adam's painting of Jesus praying alone at night, derived
from Alton S. Tobey's *Jesus in Prayer*. Sable is already developing the image
that will establish the palette.

Use the painting as atmosphere and origin, not as religious decoration.
Jesus is praying: still, listening, surrendered, in relationship with the
Father. Preserve that quiet gravity.

Palette direction:

- Moonlit indigo night.
- Cerulean depth and distant valley color.
- Moon-gold as a restrained signal, not a broad accent.
- Robe-purple as a rare secondary note.
- Deep shadow, luminous edges, and painterly transitions.

Avoid generic church imagery, glowing crosses, stock-photo spirituality,
literal halos, neon gradients, and predictable AI-tech motifs.

## The homepage as one continuous scene

There is no menu in version one. The page unfolds vertically as a single
cinematic sequence.

### Scene 1 — Presence

Open with Adam's name, space, atmosphere, and the signature line:

> **Built piece by peace.**

The first screen should feel composed rather than filled. Adam appears before
his credentials or projects.

Reserve a clean content region for a final one- or two-sentence statement
written or approved by Adam. In the concept, label this region **Statement
copy supplied by Adam**. Do not invent his testimony, biography, or spiritual
claims.

### Scene 2 — Invitation

Create one open door into the page. The tone is **come explore with me**, not
**observe my work**.

Reserve one short invitation line and label it **Invitation copy supplied by
Adam** in the concept.

The invitation may be expressed through motion, framing, scroll behavior, or a
quiet visual cue. It should not require a button-heavy call-to-action block.

### Scene 3 — The first two pieces

Introduce two permanent writings as major chapters in the same world. Do not
force a public category label yet. Internally and in URLs they may live under
`writing`; visually they are simply things Adam has written.

Do not reduce them to generic blog cards. Each should feel like a doorway with
its own visual atmosphere, title, description, and date.

1. **AI Is Not a Surprise to the Architect**  
   Description: Every tool can be turned toward good or evil. On technology,
   discernment, and the God who was holding it all together before the first
   circuit was drawn.

2. **The God of Perfect Timing**  
   Description: The delay is not the denial. On God's perfect timing, and the
   "suddenly" that is worth the wait.

The writings say something important about Adam. They are not disposable feed
items. Their length is unconstrained, they may carry images, and their URLs
should remain durable.

### Scene 4 — Horizon

Close with a restrained sense that this world is beginning rather than ending.
Moroni, future projects, additional writing, and the longer 1978-to-present arc
exist beyond version one, but do not build empty rooms for them tonight.

A simple name, line, or quiet contact path is enough. The ending should echo
the opening rather than become a conventional footer directory.

## The permanent writing pages

Routes:

- `/writing/ai-is-not-a-surprise-to-the-architect`
- `/writing/god-of-perfect-timing`

Each page should include:

- A cinematic opening image or visual field.
- Title, description, author, and date.
- Beautiful long-form reading typography.
- The complete writing with no arbitrary length limit.
- Optional inline imagery.
- One quiet return path to AdamLeipold.com.
- No conventional global navigation requirement.

The page should feel like entering a deeper chamber of the homepage's world,
not switching to a generic article template.

## Responsive and interaction direction

- Design mobile-first, with the intimacy and polish of a native iOS experience.
- Let desktop gain scale and atmosphere rather than simply widening text.
- Motion should be slow, purposeful, and optional.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep text readable against every painted or photographic field.
- Preserve keyboard focus, semantic headings, and strong contrast.
- Avoid interaction that hides the writing or requires precision gestures.

## Technical boundaries

The current repository is a dependency-free static HTML site. The two markdown
writings make a small content-aware build appropriate, but the technology
should remain subordinate to the experience.

Implementation will provide:

- A homepage route.
- A writing index internally, even if no index is visible in version one.
- Markdown source for each permanent writing.
- Deterministic static output suitable for Vercel.
- Durable slugs and metadata.
- Accessible, responsive behavior.

Do not design a CMS, account system, conversational interface, Slack workflow,
knowledge graph, or automated publishing pipeline in this pass. Those are
separate future systems.

## Content boundaries

Adam will write or approve all first-person testimony and biography.

Do not invent:

- The full 1978-to-present technology arc.
- The daily 4:30 a.m. rhythm.
- Cancer and MD Anderson details.
- Baptism by Joel Osteen at Lakewood.
- Claims about how AI accompanied him through those experiences.

The design should leave room for those stories later without requiring them
for tonight's release.

## What Sable should return

Provide a cohesive creative direction that Fable can translate into the final
UI and Lisa can implement faithfully:

1. The cinematic concept in a few sentences.
2. Homepage composition for desktop and mobile.
3. Long-form writing-page composition for desktop and mobile.
4. Final palette sampled from the prepared painting image.
5. Typography direction.
6. Image-treatment guidance for the homepage and both writings.
7. Motion and transition guidance.
8. Any required visual assets, with clear intended placement.

Favor one strong direction over several diluted alternatives.

## Version-one acceptance test

Version one succeeds when:

- The homepage feels finished with only Adam, the invitation, and two writings.
- The painting's spiritual atmosphere shapes the experience without making it
  look like institutional religious media.
- Both writings are readable, permanent, image-capable pages.
- Mobile feels designed rather than adapted.
- Nothing visible leads to an empty destination.
- The result can be deployed tonight and replaced or expanded without regret.

The standard is not "enough content." The standard is **one small world that
feels fully alive.**
